## How to use

1. Review each draft in `templates/drafts/[name].md`
2. Edit the draft text in-place if needed
3. Change **Status** below to `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`
4. Send manually per `config/email_access.md` (only when `APPROVED`)
5. After send, update status to `SENT_TOUCH_1` or `SENT_TOUCH_2`
